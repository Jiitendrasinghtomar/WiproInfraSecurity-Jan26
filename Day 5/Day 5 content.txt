symmetric key cryptography:
	- uses single key to encrypt and decrypt data.
	- ex: key of your house, same key locks the door and unlocks the door too.
	- this key is faster in nature.
	- it can be used for working with bulk data.
	
Asymmetric key cryptography:
	- it uses 2 keys 
		1. for encryption
		2. for decryption
	- ex: - a mail/leeter box having an opening for everyone to put letters into it (encrypted data), but YOU are the only one who can open theat box with your key (decryption)
	- this scenarios makes it slower in nature
	- it cannot be used for bulk data.
	
Hybrid cryptography
	- this uses the combination of symmetric & Asymmetric methods.
	
Hash function
	- cannot be reversed
	- it takes input then converts it to fixed length output.
